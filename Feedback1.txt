Goal:
Turn v0.1 from a strong playable foundation into a crisp, lively, public-facing social strategy game candidate.

Do not push anything until explicitly approved.

Critical visual standard:
The artifact must look HD, crisp, modern, and sleek.

Do not interpret “early internet,” “forum,” “social network,” or “nostalgia” as permission to make the UI muddy, blurry, cramped, low contrast, or visually dated.

The theme can be nostalgic, but the execution should feel like a premium modern browser game.

Required visual qualities:

* sharp typography
* clean spacing
* readable labels
* strong contrast
* polished hover/focus states
* smooth animations
* crisp SVG rendering
* deliberate color palette
* no blurry raster UI
* no default browser controls
* no gray-box prototype panels
* no cramped text blocks
* no fake Facebook clone styling

Brand:
Keep the game title:
The Social Engine: Context Collapse

Subtitle:
Find your people. Try not to lose the plot.

Fictional platform:
SignalBarn is acceptable and has a distinct rural/weird-internet identity. Keep it unless there is a strong reason to rename.

Do not use Facebook, Meta, Twitter/X, TikTok, Reddit, or other real platform branding, layouts, logos, icons, or trademarked visuals.

Priority 1: Make the social graph feel alive

The graph is readable, but currently too static.

Improve:

* animated node pulses when meters change
* relationship edges change color/thickness based on trust/strain
* community nodes look visually different from people nodes
* reality-friction relatives should have a subtle warning/chaos style
* close friends should feel warmer/steadier
* drama followers / viral influence should have a sharper visual treatment
* hover or click on a node should show a small profile/relationship card
* recent affected nodes should briefly glow, shake, or ripple after events

Graph should communicate:

* who matters
* who is strained
* who boosted reach
* who is causing drama
* which communities are healthy or risky
* how the player’s social engine is changing over time

Priority 2: Make the feed feel more like a feed

The main dashboard needs a more alive recent-feed panel.

Improve:

* show recent posts/events as stacked feed cards
* add small reaction/comment/share indicators
* show which audience saw the post
* show whether the post stayed in context or escaped
* add tiny fictional comments from recurring characters
* make “Cousin Reply Guy is typing…” style moments appear dynamically
* show when Aunt JPEG, Uncle Signal, or Coworker Maya reacts

The feed should not copy any real social platform. It should be a fictional SignalBarn feed.

Priority 3: Improve action cards and daily choices

Action cards should feel like strategic choices, not generic buttons.

For each action card, show:

* category
* likely upside
* likely risk
* effect tags
* viral potential
* relationship risk
* community/skill/reach focus

Example tags:
+skill
+belonging
+reach
+agency
+drama risk
+context risk
+burnout relief
+trust repair

Make selected action state visually strong.

Priority 6: Strengthen virality

Virality should feel exciting and dangerous.

Improve viral event modal:

* show viral source: community share, reply fight, algorithm bump, screenshot spread, quote-share, family argument, expert boost
* show follower breakdown changes
* show trust/drama/burnout impact
* show who benefited
* show who got annoyed
* show whether it created opportunity or only heat

Add response choices:

* clarify publicly
* move to DMs
* lean into reach
* delete post
* pin context
* mute thread
* apologize
* ask a trusted friend to help explain
* make a follow-up tutorial
* log off

Each response should have distinct effects.
