Priority 1: First-run clarity

The main dashboard has a lot of good information, but it is dense.

Improve the first-run experience so a new player immediately understands:

1. choose one action per day
2. actions affect reach, trust, agency, belonging, drama, burnout, heat, and relationships
3. the social graph shows who is affected
4. viral/context-collapse events are dangerous multipliers
5. multiple victory paths are possible

Add or improve a compact “Today’s goal” or “How this works” strip on the main dashboard.

Example:
Pick one action. Watch the graph react. Keep your people, grow your reach, and survive the feed.

Do not add a long tutorial wall.

Priority 2: Make the main dashboard easier to scan

Keep the rich UI, but improve hierarchy.

Improve:

* reduce visual clutter where possible
* make the current day/action decision more prominent
* make the feed stack easier to read
* make graph effects easier to notice
* keep victory paths visible but not overwhelming
* make failure warnings easier to understand at a glance
* make the bottom meters cleaner and more readable

The main screen should be screenshot-friendly and playable at 1440x900.

Priority 3: Social graph polish

The graph is good. Make it feel more alive and more readable.

Improve:

* avoid lower-label clustering where possible
* make affected-node animation more noticeable but not noisy
* make node categories more visually distinct
* make close ties, community nodes, risky groups, and reality-friction relatives recognizable
* make hover cards concise and useful
* make edge strain/trust styling clearer

The player should be able to tell:

* who helped
* who got annoyed
* who is strained
* who is risky
* which communities are healthy

Priority 4: Dynamic create-group forecast

Fix this:

* preview expected starting members, health, audience, risk, and likely tradeoffs
* keep the preview readable and not overly numeric

This is one of the last obvious rough edges.
